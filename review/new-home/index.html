Your coaching rules

It doesn't learn fitness. It learns you.

Tell Physiqly how you coach once. Then it watches the decisions you make and notices the patterns. When it sees one you keep repeating, it suggests it as a rule and shows you why.

  • Your philosophy and how you answer the situations clients bring you
  • The changes you approve, and the ones you edit before they go out
  • The drafts you shorten, and the alerts you decide can wait
Nothing becomes a rule until you add it.

Every suggestion shows the decisions behind it. Add it, change the number, or tell it not to ask again.

Pain and injuries always come to you.

Physiqly never gives advice on them, whatever you set. That cannot be switched off.

Straight answers

The questions trainers actually ask.

Will Physiqly change my clients' plans by itself?
No. It prepares messages and nutrition changes and explains why, but nothing reaches a client until you approve it. If the client's numbers change after it prepared something, it tells you instead of applying an old suggestion.
Is the AI going to say something daft to my client?
It only answers the situations you told it to answer, in the words you gave it. Everything else either comes to you first or gets flagged after it replies. Anything touching pain, injury or dizziness goes straight to you and cannot be switched off.
Will my clients actually use another app?
They open one screen that tells them what to do today: the workout, what is left to eat, a quick check-in. Logging a usual meal is one tap.
Do I have to move everything over by hand?
Your client list, exercises, meals and programs import from a spreadsheet, so what you already keep comes with you.
How much does it cost?
While we onboard coaches personally, your plan and price are agreed with you on the demo call, based on how many clients you coach. You are billed monthly in shekels, directly by Physiqly, and nothing is charged before we agree it. Everything in the app is included: there are no paid add-ons.
Does it really do everything, or will I still need other tools?
Training, nutrition, check-ins, progress, messaging and your client billing are all in here. That is the point of it.
