Straight answers

The questions trainers actually ask.

01Is the price going to climb the moment I grow?
The price is per tier of clients and the tiers are published. Nothing that appears in the app is a paid add-on you find out about later.
02Will my clients actually use another app?
They open one screen with today's session on it and a Start button. Last week's weights are already filled in. That is the whole ask.
03Do I have to move everything over by hand?
Exercises, meals and workouts import from a spreadsheet, so the library you already keep comes with you.
04Does it really do everything, or will I still need other tools?
Training, nutrition, supplements, weigh-ins, progress photos, messaging and your client billing are all in here. That is the point of it.
05What happens to the messages I get at eleven at night?
Clients send structured requests instead of open chat, so a question about swapping an exercise arrives as that, and it waits in one place instead of your personal phone.
