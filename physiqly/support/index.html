Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open your profile from the app, scroll to the bottom, and choose "Delete account." Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

How do I connect Apple Health or Health Connect?

You'll be prompted to allow access the first time step tracking is used in the app. You can change this permission anytime in your device's system settings.

I'm a trainer — how do I manage my subscription?

Subscription and billing details are available from your trainer profile settings.

Can I get a refund?

Within 14 days of a charge, yes — no reason needed. Where you ask depends on where you subscribed; see our Refund Policy.
